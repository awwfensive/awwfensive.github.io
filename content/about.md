I’m Aniket. I go by Awwfensive online. I break software, study how AI systems fail, and build tools for vulnerability research.

My research sits across three areas:

- **AI for Security:** autonomous vulnerability discovery, offensive agents, harness engineering, and evaluation through [Hackbot](/projects/hackbot/).
- **Security of AI:** prompt injection, tool abuse, connector authorization, agent permissions, and agent isolation.
- **Vulnerability Research:** IDOR, BFLA, access control, tenant isolation, business logic, and API security.

I work independently. This site brings together [published disclosures](/findings/), [technical analyses](/research/), and [notes](/notes/). Experiments and future research questions are described as work in progress.
